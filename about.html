Brand Philosophy

A Passion Turned into Purpose

AMAZING ROY was born from a lifelong dedication to garment construction and style. Founder Roheemot Omolara Yunus discovered her fascination with textiles and design at an early age, evolving a personal interest into a professional modest fashion atelier.

The brand name "Amazing ROY" combines personal identity with creative ambition. It serves as a commitment to empowering women — students, corps members, young professionals, and entrepreneurs — who seek wardrobe solutions that balance sophistication, comfort, and modest values.

Under Roheemot's creative direction, every bespoke garment is built around structural integrity, precise measurements, and functional elegance tailored to the client's lifestyle.

About Roheemot Omolara Yunus

Roheemot's approach to tailoring combines academic discipline with practical garment crafting. Her background in higher education provided a strong analytical foundation that she applies directly to fashion production, pattern engineering, and client consultations.

Focusing on the unique attire needs of ambitious women, she advocates for garments that feel as good as they look. Rather than following fast-fashion trends, her work emphasizes clean tailoring, quality fabrics, and versatile modest designs that seamlessly adapt from formal occasions to professional environments.
